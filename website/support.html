FAQ

Is Combat Power a real fitness metric?

No. Combat Power is an original, for-fun score that turns consistent, progressive training into a number you can chase. It is not a medical or scientific metric.

Does my Apple Health data leave my phone?

No. Apple Health data is read and used entirely on your device to compute Combat Power. It is never sent to our servers, never stored in iCloud by Reploom, and never used for ads or sold. See the privacy policy.

How does voice logging work?

Tap the mic and say something like “squat 80 kilos, 5 reps.” The audio is sent to a speech-to-text service to transcribe, then parsed into structured sets. You confirm before it saves.

Do I need an account?

No. Reploom works fully on-device with no login. Leaderboards are optional — nothing about you leaves your phone until you choose a handle.

How do I connect or disconnect Apple Health?

Open Settings → Apple Health in the app to connect, sync, or disconnect. You can also revoke access any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

How do I delete my data?

All your Reploom data lives on your device. Deleting the app removes it.
