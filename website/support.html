Quick help

A set or meal did not save

  1. Keep Reploom open and check the Log or History view before entering the item again.
  2. If a recent entry appeared, review its saved confirmation and use Edit or Undo immediately if it is wrong.
  3. If a workout AI tool is offline, use the manual workout logger. For meals, enter the name, kcal, and protein in QUICK MANUAL or repeat a previously saved meal locally.
  4. Force-quit and reopen only after checking History, so you do not create an accidental duplicate.

Voice or meal AI is unavailable

Remote AI is off by default. It may be enabled only by a user confirming they are 18 or older after reading the disclosure in Reploom Settings. These optional tools also need an active Reploom Pro entitlement, remaining AI quota, an internet connection, and the Reploom processing service. Voice transcription, workout parsing, and meal analysis use Groq. A missing purchase, exhausted quota, timeout, or provider outage does not remove your existing local logs. You can keep training with manual workout logging and record user-authored meal values with QUICK MANUAL. The v1 sportswear character is built in and does not upload a personal photo.

Undo or edit a recent entry

Use the visible Edit or Undo action on the recent confirmation card. A just-saved single manual meal also exposes Edit and Undo. For older workout entries, open History and select the relevant day. Review the final value after editing; Combat Power is recalculated from the saved activity.

Apple Health

Connect or refresh Apple Health

Open Reploom's Settings tab, find Apple Health, and choose Connect or Sync. Apple controls each requested read/write permission. Reploom cannot see whether Apple has granted individual read permissions; an empty result can mean there is no matching data or that access was not granted.

Change or revoke access

Use the Health app or iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health, select Reploom, and change the data types you allow. Revoking access stops new reads and writes but does not delete records already written to Apple Health, local Reploom history, or the last Health-derived snapshot. Use Reploom's Disconnect Apple Health action to clear that snapshot and recompute Combat Power.

Remove a workout or body value from Apple Health

Manage that record in the Apple Health app. Deleting Reploom removes its local container but does not automatically remove records that were already saved to Apple Health.

AI-assisted tools

AI parsing and nutrition values are estimates. Check exercise, load, repetitions, calories, and protein before relying on them. Reploom v1 does not offer remote photo-avatar generation; its sportswear character is built into the app. See the privacy policy's processing table for exactly what is sent.

Reploom Pro subscription and quota

Purchase, restore, or manage Reploom Pro

Open Settings → Reploom Pro. The purchase screen displays Apple's localized monthly price, the 1,000-credit billing-period allowance, and the separate 60-photo limit. Choose Restore Purchases if you already subscribed with the current Apple Account. Use Manage Subscription to view renewal or cancellation options. Deleting Reploom does not cancel the Apple subscription.

Understand AI credits

Flexible workout text uses 1 credit, typed meal estimation 2, voice transcription 3, and a meal photo 8. If a voice transcript needs flexible workout parsing, that uses 1 more credit (3–4 total), so voice recording requires at least 4 credits remaining. Credits and the photo count reset when the current paid billing period ends; unused amounts do not roll over. Devices using the same Apple subscription share the same allowance. Manual workout and meal logging, saved-meal repeat, and on-device quick parsing remain available after the limit.

Failed provider attempts are returned to the displayed allowance. Repeated failures can still trigger a separate cost-safety pause until the reset time shown in Reploom; manual and local logging remain available during that pause.

A purchase is pending or not appearing

  1. Wait for Apple to finish any approval or billing step; do not purchase repeatedly.
  2. Confirm the device is signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase.
  3. Use Restore Purchases once, then reopen Reploom if Apple reports success.
  4. If StoreKit cannot load a localized price, try again later; Reploom does not substitute a hard-coded price.

Data and deletion

  • No account: there is no Reploom login or password to delete.
  • Subscription and AI quota: delete the pseudonymous service ledger in Settings; this blocks Remote AI until the current period ends and does not cancel Apple's subscription or erase purchase history. For Apple test purchases only, a one-way daily cost-safety aggregate remains through the UTC day so deletion cannot reset provider attempts.
  • Local data: deleting Reploom from iOS removes its local app container.
  • Apple Health: disconnect in Reploom to clear its snapshot; separately revoke access or delete Health records in Apple's Health app.
  • Legacy leaderboard: v1 has no public leaderboard; prior TestFlight participants can remove the old row keyed by their random ID from Settings before deleting the app. A user-chosen handle in that row may itself be identifying.

Product FAQ

Is Combat Power a medical or scientific metric?

No. Combat Power is an original, for-fun training score. It is not a diagnosis, medical advice, a measure of personal worth, or a substitute for professional care.

Does Reploom require an account?

No Reploom login is required. The current app uses one local profile. Reploom Pro purchases are associated with your Apple Account through StoreKit. V1 has no public leaderboard. An earlier TestFlight leaderboard used a public handle and random app-generated device ID, not an account credential; its legacy row is removable from Settings.

Does Apple Health data go to the AI service?

No. AI requests contain the selected workout text, voice clip, meal text, or meal photo. A workout parse also includes the display unit and exercise catalog names/IDs needed to match the movement. Reploom does not attach HealthKit records to those requests.

Can I use Reploom offline?

Local history, manual workout logging, direct manual meal logging, and saved-meal repeats are designed to work from on-device data. When a network is available, Reploom may refresh its public exercise catalog in the background; a failed refresh falls back to the validated on-device catalog and does not block local logging. Voice transcription, AI parsing, and meal analysis need Remote AI consent, an active Reploom Pro entitlement, remaining quota, and a network connection, and may be unavailable during a provider outage.

A useful report should include:

  • Reploom version and build number;
  • iPhone model and iOS version;
  • the screen and action that failed;
  • the exact message shown; and
  • whether the issue happens again after reopening the app.

Do not email Health records, diagnoses, full meal photos, voice recordings, passwords, or other sensitive data unless support specifically explains why it is necessary.

Support messages are handled in Gmail and retained only as long as needed to resolve the request, protect the service, or meet a legal obligation. They are manually deleted when no longer needed and may be deleted earlier on request unless security or law requires retention.
