5. Reploom Pro monthly subscription

Reploom Pro is an auto-renewable monthly subscription purchased through Apple's In-App Purchase system. The app displays the localized product name, monthly period, and price returned by StoreKit before you confirm. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation. The subscription renews automatically for another month unless you cancel it in your Apple Account subscription settings before renewal. Apple controls billing, renewal, cancellation, and eligible refunds.

Each paid billing period includes 1,000 AI credits and no more than 60 meal-photo analyses. A flexible workout-text parse uses 1 credit, typed-meal estimation 2, voice transcription 3, and a meal-photo analysis 8. A voice transcript that needs the flexible workout parser uses 1 additional credit (3–4 total); the app requires 4 remaining credits before recording. Unused credits and photos do not roll over. Multiple devices using the same Apple subscription share one allowance. Manual workout logging, common on-device quick parsing, saved-meal repeat, plans, history, body map, Combat Power, and Apple Health features do not require Reploom Pro.

Provider failures are returned to the displayed successful-use allowance. Separate cost-safety counters do not refund failed attempts: remote AI may pause after 1,250 weighted attempts or 75 meal-photo attempts in a billing period. Apple test entitlements also have a 200-weighted-attempt and 12-photo UTC-day ceiling because test billing periods renew on an accelerated schedule. These safeguards do not block manual or local logging.

Use Restore Purchases in Reploom to ask Apple to restore an eligible entitlement, or use the manage-subscription link to change or cancel renewal. Deleting the app, disabling Remote AI, or deleting Reploom's quota data does not cancel the Apple subscription. Deleting the quota data blocks Remote AI until the current paid period ends so deletion cannot reset the allowance or provider-cost safeguards; see the Data Deletion Guide.
