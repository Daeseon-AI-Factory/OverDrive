Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-09

One-line summary: Your Apple Health data never leaves your device. Your workout/meal inputs are sent only when you ask us to structure them, and an avatar photo is sent only after you explicitly confirm AI processing and tap Generate. There are no accounts, no ads, no third-party trackers, and we never sell, rent, or mine your data.

1. Who we are

Reploom is an independent fitness app. For any privacy question or request, contact support@reploom.app.

2. Apple Health / HealthKit data

If you connect Apple Health, Reploom reads these data types on your device to calculate your Combat Power score and show progress:

  • Workouts
  • Active energy burned
  • Heart rate
  • Resting heart rate
  • VO₂ max
  • Body mass (weight)
  • Body fat percentage

When you log a strength workout or body-composition entry, Reploom can write these back to Apple Health so your real training is reflected there:

  • Workouts
  • Body mass (weight)
  • Body fat percentage
  • Lean body mass

Apple Health data is processed entirely on your device. It is never transmitted to our servers or any third party, never stored in iCloud by Reploom, and never used for advertising, marketing, data-mining, or sale. Reploom only writes data you actually logged — never fabricated or “game” numbers.

3. Data that is sent off your device (and why)

Reploom has no health-data backend. Information leaves your device only when you ask the app to interpret a log or explicitly generate a photo-based avatar. That input goes to our processing endpoint, which forwards it to a third-party AI service to convert it into structured data, then returns the result:

What you doWhat is sentWho processes itPurpose
Type a workout/mealThe text you wroteGroq, or Google Gemini (fallback)Parse into sets / nutrition
Speak a workout logThe audio you recordedGroq (Whisper)Speech-to-text
Pick a meal photo / Generate a sportswear avatarThe image you choseGoogle GeminiEstimate nutrition / generate an avatar

Selecting an avatar photo stores it locally but does not upload it. Before generation, the app asks you to confirm that you are 18 or older, that the photo is yours to use, and that you consent to Google AI processing. Inputs are sent only to return your result. We do not attach your identity to them (Reploom has no account) and do not use them for advertising or tracking profiles. We never send your Apple Health data, contacts, or location through this path.

4. Optional leaderboard

Leaderboards are opt-in. Until you choose a handle, nothing about you leaves your device. If you opt in, the app may send a handle you type, a random anonymous device ID (not tied to your Apple ID, name, email, or phone), and an optional crew code.

5. On-device storage

Your workouts, sets, cardio, body-composition entries, daily goals, food logs, Combat Power history, and settings are stored locally on your device. Avatar originals and generated avatar files are stored in the app's private documents directory and can be deleted together from the avatar controls. Deleting the app removes all local data.

6. What we do NOT do

  • No advertising and no ad networks
  • No third-party analytics or trackers
  • No selling, renting, or data-mining of your information
  • No sending your Apple Health data off your device
  • No storing your Apple Health data in iCloud
  • No accounts, no email/phone collection
  • Not directed to children under 13

8. Combat Power is for fun

Combat Power is an original, for-fun score — not a medical or scientific metric — and is labeled as such in the app.

9. Your choices

  • Revoke Health, Microphone, or Photos access in iOS Settings.
  • Skip the optional leaderboard (default).
  • Delete an avatar original and generated avatar together from the avatar controls.
  • Delete the app to remove on-device data.

10. Changes & contact

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above and post the new version here. Questions: support@reploom.app.
