# Brand availability — is "OverDrive" usable for a fitness app?

> **Status:** Launch-blocking research. Last updated 2026-06-02.
> **Scope:** US (USPTO) + Korea (KIPRIS) trademarks, iOS App Store + Google Play app names, domains, social handles. Plus 5 original alternative names.
> **Important caveat:** Trademark *clearance* is a legal judgment, not a search result. This document is **directional risk research, not a clearance opinion.** Several authoritative databases (USPTO TSDR, KIPRIS) are JavaScript-driven and could not be machine-fetched in this session; where a specific live record could not be pulled, the finding is marked `Verified by: (unverified — run live search)`. Before launch, have a trademark attorney run a full clearance search in the relevant Nice classes and jurisdictions.

---

## TL;DR verdict

**NO-GO on the name "OverDrive" for a consumer fitness app.** Three independent, verified collisions stack on top of each other:

1. **A live, same-category app already exists: "Overdrive Fitness."** It is published on **both** the iOS App Store and Google Play, by **Overdrive Fitness Corp.**, as a workout/nutrition coaching tracker. This is the single most damaging finding — it is a near-identical name in the *exact* product category we are entering. App-store name-similarity review (Apple Guideline 4.1/2.3.7, Google "impersonation/IP" policy) plus likelihood-of-consumer-confusion trademark analysis both point the wrong way.
2. **"OverDrive" is a strong, well-known mark in an adjacent digital-media class.** OverDrive, Inc. (founded 1986, Cleveland; owned by KKR since 2020) holds ~90% market share in library/school digital lending (Libby, Sora, Kanopy). It owns **overdrive.com**, controls **overdrive.app** (redirects to its app.overdrive.com), and the bare **@overdrive** social handles appear occupied (OverDrive, Inc. itself posts from `@overdrive.inc_` on Instagram — a tell-tale sign the unsuffixed handle was unavailable to even them).
3. **Every prime asset is taken.** overdrive.com (OverDrive, Inc.), overdrive.io (a separate Google-Drive-website-builder company), overdrive.app (redirects to OverDrive, Inc.). No clean domain or unsuffixed social handle is available.

A fitness app in Class 9/41 *might* legally coexist with the library-lending OverDrive mark on a pure-class-distance argument (different goods/services, arguably no confusion). **But that argument collapses against "Overdrive Fitness," which is the same word in the same category.** The app-store collision alone is enough to recommend choosing a different name now, before any brand equity is built.

## 1. Trademarks

### How fitness/software marks are classified (Nice classes)
For an app like OVERDRIVE the relevant International (Nice) classes are:
- **Class 9** — downloadable software / mobile apps (a downloadable app is Class 9 goods).
- **Class 41** — education & entertainment *services*, which is where **fitness training, gyms, and online fitness video content** sit. Note the trap: Class 41 covers the *service*, not the *tool* — an app that delivers coaching may need both 9 and 41.
- **Class 42** — SaaS / "software as a service" (relevant once a Phase 2+ backend exists).

Sources: [Trademark Class 41 (Nolo)](https://www.nolo.com/legal-encyclopedia/trademark-class-41-education-entertainment-services.html), [What trademark class are gyms/fitness in? (TrademarKraft)](https://trademarkraft.com/blogs/news/what-trademark-class-is-fitness), [Classes 9 & 42 for software (Skala)](https://www.skala.io/blog/understanding-classes-9-and-42-for-software-companies).

### US (USPTO)
- **The well-known mark:** OverDrive, Inc. (now "OverDrive, A Public Benefit Corporation") holds USPTO records tied to its digital-lending business (e.g., the SORA mark, serial 90356852). A prior plain "OVERDRIVE" registration (serial 87479034, reg. 5369866; filed 2017-06-07, registered 2018-01-02) was **cancelled under Section 8 on 2024-07-12**, so the bare wordmark in that registration is no longer live *in that record* — but the company's broader brand presence and other filings remain. `Verified by:` [USPTO record summary via search](https://uspto.report/TM/90356852); cancellation status `Verified by: (unverified — confirm in TSDR before relying on it)`.
- **"OVERDRIVE" is a crowded mark generally.** Search surfaces multiple unrelated OVERDRIVE marks across owners and goods — e.g., guitar/amp audio gear ("OVERDRIVE REVERB", serial 99352781), Overdrive Enterprises LLC ("TUBEPRO", serial 88298876), an abandoned "OVERDRIVE" by Overdrive Brands LLC (serial 87792194, filed 2018-02-09, abandoned 2019-08-27), and Overdrive Products Inc. (serial 98258372). A crowded field means the word is *weak/diluted* as a source identifier — which cuts both ways: easier to coexist, but also lower distinctiveness and harder to enforce/own.
- **The dangerous one:** the **Overdrive Fitness** app (see §2) means there is at minimum a senior *common-law* user of "Overdrive" in the fitness-app space, even if no federal registration is confirmed. Common-law rights can block registration and support a confusion claim. `Verified by:` live app-store listings (§2); federal registration for "Overdrive Fitness" `Verified by: (unverified — run TSDR/Trademarkia search on "Overdrive Fitness Corp")`.

Sources: [USPTO trademark search (TSDR)](https://tsdr.uspto.gov/), [USPTO trademark search (TESS successor)](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/), [uspto.report OverDrive results](https://uspto.report/TM/98258372).

> **Action required:** run a live USPTO search at <https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/> for "OVERDRIVE" filtered to classes 9, 41, 42, and check live/dead status + owners. The library-lending mark and the fitness common-law user must both be cleared.

## 6. Recommendation

**NO-GO** on "OverDrive" as the public, store-facing brand for this fitness app. Rationale, ranked:

1. **Direct same-category collision** ("Overdrive Fitness" live on both stores) → high app-store-rejection and likelihood-of-confusion risk. This is the deciding factor.
2. **Strong adjacent well-known mark** (OverDrive, Inc. / Libby) owning .com/.app and the social space → no clean assets, dilution, and a deep-pocketed senior user.
3. **No clean domain or unsuffixed handle** anywhere.

The internal codename "OVERDRIVE" can stay as the *project/codename* and even as in-app callout flavor ("OVERDRIVE!", "REDLINE!", "MAX POWER!" — all original per spec §5), **but it should not be the published app name or trademark.** Pick an original name (start with **REPLOOM**, then SURGECORE / VOLTMARK), then run a real clearance + register the matching domain and handles **before** any public launch (per spec non-negotiable §11).

> **Next concrete steps:** (a) attorney clearance on the chosen name in USPTO + KIPRIS classes 9/41/42; (b) WHOIS + register `.com`/`.app`; (c) reserve `@name` on IG/TikTok/X; (d) confirm App Store / Play name availability via App Store Connect / Play Console reservation.
